## Ingredients 
- 8 Tbsp (1 stick) butter, room temperature
- ¾ cup creamy peanut butter
- ½ cup packed brown sugar
- ⅔ cup granulated sugar, divided (⅓ cup for dough, ⅓ cup for rolling)
-  1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1 large egg, room temperature
- 1¼ cups all-purpose flour
- ½ tsp baking soda
- ¼ tsp kosher salt
- 32 chocolate kisses (or mini reeses cups)

---
## Directions
1. Whisk flour and baking soda together in a medium bowl. Set aside.
2. Beat together butter, peanut butter, brown sugar, ⅓ cup of the granulated sugar, and salt until well-combined and slightly lighter in color.
3. Beat in the egg and vanilla. Then add the flour mixture and mix until just combined.
4. If the dough is too sticky to roll, refrigerate for 15 minutes.
5. Put the remaining ⅓ cup granulated sugar in a bowl. Roll dough into balls, roll in the sugar, and arrange on a baking sheet.
6. Bake at 375°F for 8–10 minutes, until they look puffy and crackled and are just starting to brown on the bottom.
7. Immediately press one chocolate kiss on top of each cookie and allow to cool to doneness.

---
## Notes
- I think I used sallys chocolate buttercream for the legs of the spiders
- when using reeces peanut butter cups, allow the cookies to cool a little before putting the reeses on. Otherwise they melt too much
