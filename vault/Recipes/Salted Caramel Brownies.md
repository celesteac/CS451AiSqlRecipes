# Salted Caramel Brownies

Rich, ultra-fudgy chocolate brownies layered and swirled with homemade salted caramel and topped with flaky sea salt.

---

### Equipment
- 9x9-inch (or 8x8-inch) metal baking pan
- Parchment paper
- Medium saucepan (for caramel)
- Heatproof mixing bowl

---

### Ingredients

#### Salted Caramel Sauce
- 1 cup (200g) granulated sugar
- 6 tbsp (85g) unsalted butter, cut into cubes and at room temperature
- 1/2 cup (120ml) heavy cream, room temperature
- 1 tsp flaky sea salt (such as Maldon)

#### Brownie Batter
- 1 cup (226g / 2 sticks) unsalted butter
- 8 oz (225g) semi-sweet or bittersweet chocolate, roughly chopped
- 1 cup (200g) granulated sugar
- 1/2 cup (100g) light brown sugar, packed
- 4 large eggs, room temperature
- 1 tsp pure vanilla extract
- 1 cup (120g) all-purpose flour
- 1/3 cup (35g) Dutch-processed cocoa powder
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt
- Flaky sea salt, for finishing

---

### Instructions

#### 1. Make the Salted Caramel
1. Heat granulated sugar in a medium saucepan over medium-low heat, stirring constantly with a heatproof spatula or wooden spoon. The sugar will form clumps and eventually melt into a thick amber-colored liquid (keep a close eye so it doesn't burn).
2. Once the sugar is completely melted, carefully add the cubed butter. Whisk vigorously until the butter is fully melted and incorporated (the mixture will bubble vigorously).
3. Slowly drizzle in the heavy cream while continuing to whisk. Allow the mixture to boil for 1 minute.
4. Remove from heat and stir in the 1 tsp flaky sea salt. Set aside to cool slightly while you make the brownie batter.

#### 2. Make the Brownie Batter
1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C). Line a 9x9-inch metal baking pan with parchment paper, leaving an overhang on the sides for easy removal.
2. In a heatproof bowl set over a pot of simmering water (or in 30-second bursts in the microwave), melt the butter and chopped chocolate together until smooth and glossy. Set aside to cool slightly.
3. In a large bowl, whisk together the granulated sugar, brown sugar, eggs, and vanilla extract vigorously for 1–2 minutes until pale, frothy, and slightly thickened (this creates the crinkly top).
4. Slowly pour the melted chocolate-butter mixture into the egg mixture, whisking constantly until combined.
5. Sift in the flour, cocoa powder, and salt. Gently fold with a rubber spatula just until no dry flour streaks remain (do not overmix).

#### 3. Assemble & Bake
1. Pour roughly half of the brownie batter into the prepared baking pan and smooth into an even layer.
2. Drizzle about 1/3 to 1/2 cup of the salted caramel sauce evenly over the batter layer.
3. Pour the remaining brownie batter over the top, gently smoothing it out.
4. Spoon additional tablespoons of caramel over the top layer and use a butter knife or toothpick to gently swirl the caramel into the top batter. *(Save any leftover caramel for serving).*
5. Bake at 350°F (175°C) for 32–38 minutes, until the edges are set and a toothpick inserted into the center comes out with moist, fudgy crumbs (not wet batter).
6. Remove from the oven and immediately sprinkle generously with flaky sea salt.
7. Allow the brownies to cool completely in the pan at room temperature before slicing for clean cuts.

---

### Baker's Notes & Tips
- **Room Temperature Dairy:** Ensure your butter and heavy cream for the caramel are at room temperature to avoid seizing the melted sugar.
- **Clean Slices:** For ultra-clean bakery-style cuts, chill the fully-cooled brownies in the refrigerator for 1–2 hours before slicing with a sharp chef's knife wiped clean between cuts.
- **Pan Type:** Use a light-colored metal pan rather than glass, as glass retains heat unevenly and can cause the edges to overbake before the center sets.
