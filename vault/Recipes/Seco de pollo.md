## Ingredients 
- 3 chicken breasts
- some potatos (1 bag small, 6 large?)
- half onion
- half bell pepper
- half bag of spinach
- two bundles of cilantro
- bad of frozen vegetables
- rice to serve
Materials
- large pot
- blender



---
## Directions
1. Peel potatos
2. Blend half an onion, half bell pepper, spinach, and a lot of cilantro (with a little water)
3. Put the mixture in a large pot. Put a lid on the pot and turn the heat to medium. Let the mixture evaporate and collect 
4. Add chicken. Cook for 5 min total and flip to cook both sides
5. Add water
6. Salt
7. Taste
8. Potatos, peas, carrots, corns, veggies, etc
9. Cook some more (40 min - 1 hr)
10. Serve with rice and optionally beans
