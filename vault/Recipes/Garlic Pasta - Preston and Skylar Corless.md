## Ingredients 
- 1 lb. Pasta
- 1/4 cup Olive oil
- 1/4 cup Butter
- 1 egg
- 1/2 cup Parmesan cheese
- 2 teaspoons Garlic Salt
- 1 tsp. Parsley(optional)

---
## Directions
Boil pasta in salted water (think ocean) and 2Tbs. Olive oil according to time directions.  Drain return to hot pot. Add egg, cook and stir. Add butter and remaining olive oil. Add Parmesan cheese. Add garlic salt to taste. Parsley (optional).
