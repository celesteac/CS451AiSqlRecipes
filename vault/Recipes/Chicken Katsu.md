## Ingredients 
- 2 chicken breast
- flour
- egg
- Panko 
- frying oil


---
## Directions
1. Cut the chicken into filles and pound them
2. Coat the chicken in flour, then egg, then panko 
3. Deep fry for a couple of minutes until crispy
4. For meal prep, place the un-fried chicken cutlets between layers of parchment paper and freeze. You can fry them from frozen later
