## Ingredients

### Dough
- 1/3 cup whole milk, warmed (about 110°F)
- 1 packet (2 1/4 tsp) active dry yeast
- 1/4 cup granulated sugar, divided
- 4 tbsp unsalted butter, melted and cooled slightly
- 2 large eggs, at room temperature
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 2 3/4 to 3 cups all-purpose flour
- 1/2 tsp salt

### Orange Cinnamon Filling
- 1/2 cup granulated sugar
- 1/4 cup packed light brown sugar
- 1 1/2 tbsp ground cinnamon
- Zest of 2 large fresh oranges
- 4 tbsp unsalted butter, melted

### Orange Glaze
- 1 cup powdered sugar
- 2–3 tbsp freshly squeezed orange juice
- 1/2 tsp fresh orange zest
- 1/2 tsp vanilla extract

---

## Instructions

### 1. Make the Dough
- In the bowl of a stand mixer, combine the warm milk, yeast, and 1 teaspoon of the sugar. Let sit for 5–10 minutes until foamy.
- Add the remaining sugar, melted butter, eggs, and vanilla extract. Whisk to combine.
- Add 2 3/4 cups of flour and the salt. Using the dough hook attachment, mix on low speed until a soft dough forms.
- Knead on medium-low speed for 5–7 minutes (or knead by hand on a lightly floured surface) until smooth and elastic. If too sticky, add the remaining 1/4 cup of flour 1 tablespoon at a time.
- Shape the dough into a ball and place it in an oiled bowl, turning once to grease the top. Cover with plastic wrap or a clean towel and let rise in a warm place until doubled in size, about 1 hour.

### 2. Prepare the Filling & Shape
- Butter and lightly flour a 9x5-inch loaf pan.
- In a small bowl, rub the orange zest into the granulated sugar and brown sugar using your fingers until fragrant. Stir in the cinnamon.
- Punch down the dough and turn it out onto a lightly floured surface. Roll into approximately a 12x20-inch rectangle.
- Brush the surface evenly with the 4 tablespoons of melted butter, then sprinkle the orange-cinnamon sugar evenly over the butter.
- Cut the dough lengthwise into 6 equal strips. Stack the strips on top of each other, then cut the stack into 6 equal squares/rectangles (yielding 6 stacks of layered squares).
- Layer the dough squares cut-side up (like index cards in a file box) into the prepared loaf pan.
- Cover loosely and let rise again for 30–45 minutes, until puffy.

### 3. Bake & Glaze
- Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C).
- Bake the loaf for 30–35 minutes, until golden brown on top and baked through the center. (If the top browns too quickly, tent loosely with aluminum foil for the last 10 minutes).
- Let the bread cool in the pan on a wire rack for 10–15 minutes.
- Meanwhile, whisk together the powdered sugar, orange juice, orange zest, and vanilla extract in a small bowl until smooth.
- Drizzle the glaze generously over the warm bread and serve warm.

---

## Notes & Tips
- **Zest First:** Always zest your oranges before juicing them for the glaze.
- **Serving:** Pull apart the individual warm layers with your fingers for the best texture and experience.
