# Stovetop Popcorn

A classic, foolproof method for making light, crispy, and perfectly popped popcorn on the stove without burnt kernels.

---

## Equipment
- Large, heavy-bottomed pot with a lid (approx. 3–4 quarts)
- Large serving bowl

---

## Ingredients

### Popcorn Base
- 2–3 tbsp high-smoke point oil (such as coconut oil, avocado oil, or canola oil)
- 1/3 cup high-quality popping corn kernels

### Seasoning & Toppings (to taste)
- 2–3 tbsp unsalted butter, melted (optional)
- 1/2–1 tsp fine sea salt or popcorn salt
- *Optional flavorings:* Nutritional yeast, smoked paprika, garlic powder, or grated parmesan

---

## Instructions

1. **Test the Oil:**
   - Add the oil and 3–4 "test" kernels to the pot.
   - Cover with the lid and place over medium-high heat.

2. **Wait for the Test Kernels:**
   - When the test kernels pop, the oil is at the ideal temperature. Remove the popped test kernels with a spoon or tongs.

3. **Even Heating:**
   - Add the remaining 1/3 cup of kernels in a single, even layer.
   - Cover the pot and immediately remove it from the heat for **30 seconds** (this brings all kernels up to near-popping temperature so they pop at the same time and don't burn).

4. **Pop the Corn:**
   - Return the pot to medium-high heat.
   - Keep the lid slightly cracked/ajar to allow steam to escape (this keeps the popcorn light and crisp rather than chewy).
   - Shake the pot gently back and forth over the burner while the corn pops.

5. **Remove from Heat:**
   - Once the popping slows to roughly 2–3 seconds between pops, remove the pot immediately from the heat.
   - Remove the lid and dump the popcorn into your serving bowl right away to prevent trapped steam or scorching at the bottom.

6. **Season and Serve:**
   - Drizzle warm melted butter over the top and toss thoroughly.
   - Sprinkle fine salt and desired seasonings, tossing gently until evenly coated.

---

## Notes & Tips

- **Vent Steam:** Leaving the lid slightly cracked while popping allows steam to escape, which is the secret to extra-crispy stovetop popcorn.
- **Fine Salt:** Use fine table salt or grind sea salt with a mortar and pestle or spice grinder so it clings to the kernels better.
- **Oil Choice:** Coconut oil gives the most authentic movie-theater flavor, while ghee or clarified butter adds a rich, buttery taste without burning.
