## Ingredients
- 3 ripe bananas, mashed
- 1/3 cup butter, melted
- 3/4 cup brown sugar
- 1 large egg
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 1/4 teaspoon salt
- 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
## Directions
1. Preheat the oven to 350°F and grease a 9x5-inch loaf pan.
2. Stir the melted butter into the mashed bananas, then mix in the sugar, egg, and vanilla.
3. Sprinkle the baking soda and salt over the mixture, then fold in the flour until just combined.
4. Pour into the pan and bake 55 to 65 minutes, until a toothpick comes out clean.
## Notes
- Freeze overripe bananas in their peels and thaw them for this.
