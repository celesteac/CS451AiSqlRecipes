## Ingredients
**Crust**
- 1 cup butter, softened
- 1/2 cup granulated sugar
- 2 cups all-purpose flour
**Filling**
- 4 large eggs
- 1 1/2 cups granulated sugar
- 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
- 2 lemons, juiced and zested
- powdered sugar for dusting
## Directions
1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Press the butter, sugar, and flour together into a 9x13-inch pan and bake 15 to 20 minutes.
2. Whisk the eggs, sugar, flour, lemon juice, and zest until smooth.
3. Pour over the hot crust and bake 20 minutes more, until set.
4. Cool completely, chill, then dust with powdered sugar and cut into bars.
