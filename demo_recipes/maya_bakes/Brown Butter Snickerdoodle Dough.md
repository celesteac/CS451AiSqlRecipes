## Ingredients
- 1 cup butter, browned and cooled
- 1 1/2 cups granulated sugar
- 2 large eggs
- 2 3/4 cups all-purpose flour
- 2 teaspoons cream of tartar
- 1 teaspoon baking soda
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 2 tablespoons cinnamon sugar for rolling
## Directions
1. Brown the butter and let it cool until it is soft but not liquid.
2. Beat the butter and sugar, then add the eggs.
3. Mix in the dry ingredients, roll into balls, and coat in cinnamon sugar.
