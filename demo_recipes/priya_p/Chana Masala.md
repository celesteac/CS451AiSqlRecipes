## Ingredients
- 2 tablespoons olive oil
- 1 onion, diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tablespoon ginger, grated
- 2 teaspoons garam masala
- 1 teaspoon cumin
- 1 can (14 oz) diced tomatoes
- 2 cans chickpeas, drained
- 1 teaspoon salt
- cilantro and rice to serve
## Directions
1. Cook the onion in oil until golden, then add the garlic and ginger.
2. Stir in the garam masala and cumin for 30 seconds.
3. Add the tomatoes and chickpeas and simmer 20 minutes, mashing a few chickpeas to thicken.
4. Season with salt and serve over rice with cilantro.
