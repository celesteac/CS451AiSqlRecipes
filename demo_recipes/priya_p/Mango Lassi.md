## Ingredients
- 1 1/2 cups mango, chopped
- 1 cup plain yogurt
- 1/2 cup milk
- 2 tablespoons granulated sugar
- pinch of cardamom
## Directions
1. Blend everything until smooth.
2. Taste, add more sugar if needed, and serve cold.
