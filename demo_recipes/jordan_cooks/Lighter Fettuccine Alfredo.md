## Ingredients
- 12 ounces fettuccine
- 2 tablespoons butter
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 1/2 cups milk
- 1 tablespoon all-purpose flour
- 3/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese
- salt and pepper to taste
## Directions
1. Cook the pasta in salted water and reserve a cup of the water.
2. Melt the butter, cook the garlic, then whisk in the flour and milk until thick.
3. Stir in the Parmesan and toss with the pasta, loosening with pasta water.
