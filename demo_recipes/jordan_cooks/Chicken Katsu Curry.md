## Ingredients
- 2 chicken breast
- 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
- 2 large eggs
- 1 1/2 cups panko
- frying oil
- 1 onion, sliced
- 2 carrots, chopped
- 1 potato, cubed
- 1 box Japanese curry roux
- rice to serve
## Directions
1. Make the katsu as in Celeste's Chicken Katsu.
2. Saute the onion, carrot, and potato, cover with water, and simmer until tender.
3. Turn off the heat and dissolve the curry roux, then simmer until thick.
4. Slice the katsu and serve over rice with the curry.
## Notes
- Adapted from Celeste's katsu. The curry makes it a full dinner.
