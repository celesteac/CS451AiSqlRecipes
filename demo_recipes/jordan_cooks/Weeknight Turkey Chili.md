## Ingredients
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1 lb ground turkey
- 1 onion, diced
- 1 bell pepper, diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tablespoons chili powder
- 1 teaspoon cumin
- 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
- 2 cans kidney beans, drained
- salt to taste
## Directions
1. Brown the turkey in the oil, then add the onion, bell pepper, and garlic and cook until soft.
2. Stir in the chili powder and cumin for one minute.
3. Add the tomatoes and beans, then simmer 30 minutes, stirring now and then.
4. Season with salt and serve with cheese and sour cream.
