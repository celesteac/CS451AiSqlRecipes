## Ingredients
- 8 ounces rice noodles
- 3 tablespoons tamarind paste
- 3 tablespoons fish sauce
- 3 tablespoons brown sugar
- 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 large eggs
- 2 cups bean sprouts
- 1/3 cup peanuts, chopped
- 1 lime, cut in wedges
## Directions
1. Soak the noodles in warm water until bendy, then drain.
2. Whisk the tamarind, fish sauce, and sugar for the sauce.
3. Stir-fry the garlic in oil, push aside, and scramble the eggs.
4. Add the noodles and sauce and toss until absorbed, then add the sprouts.
5. Top with peanuts and serve with lime.
