## Ingredients
- 3 ripe avocados
- 1 lime, juiced
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
- 1/4 onion, finely diced
- 1 jalapeno, seeded and minced
- 2 tablespoons cilantro, chopped
- 1 tomato, diced
## Directions
1. Mash the avocados with the lime juice and salt.
2. Fold in the onion, jalapeno, cilantro, and tomato.
3. Taste, adjust the salt and lime, and serve right away.
